Two Lost People

No expectations. Just late nights and long conversations and something that felt impossible to name. And somehow... that became everything.

Chapter II

Four Months Away

Two months in, I had to leave for China. A long business trip. Months away from Riyadh — from her. She was devastated when she heard. She never liked long distance. She had every reason to walk away. But she didn't. She stayed on the other end of every call, every message, every silence. Through time zones and uncertainty and a situation that had no name yet. She just... stayed.

Chapter III

The Break & The Return

Then something happened. A break. One week. The longest week. I thought I'd lost her. But when we found our way back to each other, something had shifted. The fog cleared. We weren't in a situationship anymore — we were in love. Actually, properly, undeniably in love. I came back to Riyadh. Still busy, still traveling to Jeddah constantly for work, barely any time for calls. I was terrified she'd finally have enough. She didn't. She never did.

Chapter IV

Board Games & First Sight

Two months after I got back to Saudi — that's when we finally met. Actually met. In person. I took her to a board games place. It was one of those nights that felt easy in a way you can't manufacture, like we'd already spent years together. She was stunning. She laughed and it felt like something clicking into place. After that we saw each other a few more times. Nothing huge — just being near each other, which turned out to be everything.

Chapter V

The Pink Apartment

She moved into a new apartment. Tiny. Pink. Perfect. She hated the bugs. Every time she wanted to visit, I'd come first and clear it out — because of course I would. We painted the walls baby pink together. We could've done it in a day. I made it take longer on purpose. I was having the best time of my life. We'd cuddle on that awful little mattress. Slept on it once. It was genuinely terrible. I'd do it a thousand times over. Then the real furniture: a bed, a couch, a date to IKEA. She went to Jeddah to visit her family. While she was gone, I sorted her giant closet. Had it built. Had someone spray for bugs. Made it a home before she came back. Because that's what you do for someone who stayed.
